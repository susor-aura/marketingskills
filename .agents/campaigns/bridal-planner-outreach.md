# NÉVUE Bridal — Wedding Planner Outreach Script
**Campaign:** Bridal Vitality Program Beta  
**Audience:** Wedding planners — Tampa Bay / St. Pete  
**Platforms:** LinkedIn, Instagram DM  
**Goal:** 5 info calls → 3 beta planners  
**Created:** 2026-04-21

---

## Campaign Brief

| | |
|---|---|
| **Offer** | Free "Bridal Vitality Program" beta — complimentary IV sessions for the bride + up to two bridal party members |
| **Value to planner** | Differentiates their packages, adds a luxury touchpoint, zero cost or risk during beta |
| **Ask** | 15-minute call — not a sales call, a conversation |
| **Tone** | Warm, peer-to-peer. Not salesy. |
| **Scarcity** | Three beta spots, stated plainly |

---

## Before You Record: Personalization Framework

Spend 90 seconds on their profile before hitting record. Find one of these:

| Signal | What to reference |
|---|---|
| Recent wedding they posted | "I saw the Vinoy shoot you did last month — the ceremony light was incredible." |
| Their aesthetic / style | "Your editorial style is exactly the kind of bride we're building NÉVUE around." |
| Their market position | "You've clearly built the go-to luxury planning studio in St. Pete." |
| Mutual connection | "We're both connected to [name] — she thought we should meet." |
| Their team / growth | "I noticed you brought on a second planner this season — congrats." |

One specific detail beats any amount of flattery. If you can't find one, don't record yet — look harder.

## LinkedIn Video DM Script

**Target length:** 45–55 seconds  
**Tone:** Warm peer, not vendor

---

> *[Look directly at camera. Calm, unhurried.]*
>
> "Hi [Name] — I came across your work planning [specific wedding / venue] and I wanted to reach out personally.
>
> My name is [Your Name]. I'm launching NÉVUE — a luxury IV therapy concierge designed specifically for brides and bridal parties here in Tampa Bay.
>
> We're doing a small beta program called the Bridal Vitality Program — where we partner with a handful of planners before our public launch to offer their brides complimentary sessions. Our nurses come to the venue, handle everything, and the bride walks down the aisle hydrated, calm, and completely herself.
>
> I'm looking for three planners in the Tampa Bay area who want to bring something genuinely different to their brides before anyone else has it.
>
> No cost, no obligation. Just a fifteen-minute call to see if it makes sense.
>
> I'll send a note below — would love to find a time."

---

**Delivery notes:**
- Speak at 80% of your normal pace. Rushing reads as nervous or salesy.
- Hold eye contact with the lens, not the screen.
- Record in a clean, well-lit space — the visual signals luxury before you say a word.
- End by looking at the camera and pausing one beat before stopping the recording.

## Instagram DM Video Script

**Target length:** 30–40 seconds  
**Tone:** A notch warmer — this is a visual platform; lead with the visual world

---

> *[Slightly more relaxed. Warmth is non-negotiable.]*
>
> "Hi [Name] — love what you've been putting out. [Specific reference.]
>
> I'm [Your Name], launching a luxury IV concierge called NÉVUE for brides in Tampa Bay. Think: a nurse who comes to the venue, handles the whole experience, and your bride feels effortlessly herself all day.
>
> I'm doing a small beta — offering free sessions to a few select planners' brides before we open publicly. Three spots.
>
> Would love to tell you more. Mind if I send you the details?"

---

**Why shorter on Instagram:** DM video previews auto-play muted. The first visual frame and the first five seconds of speech need to earn the unmute. Keep it tight.

## Follow-Up Text

Send as a written note immediately after the video on both platforms:

> "Left you a short video above — the TLDR is: I'm launching a luxury bridal IV concierge in Tampa Bay and running a free beta for a small group of local planners before we go public. Three spots, complimentary for your brides, no strings. Happy to jump on a fifteen-minute call this week if it sounds interesting."

## Objection Responses

**"What exactly is IV therapy?"**
> "It's direct-to-bloodstream hydration, B vitamins, and a calm-formula magnesium blend — administered by a licensed RN at the venue. Most brides finish in 45–60 minutes and say it's the single thing they wish they'd known about sooner."

**"My brides might be nervous about needles."**
> "Totally valid concern — it's our most common one. Our nurses use vein mapping technology before placing a single needle, and our no-bruise rate is something we built the entire service around. Happy to walk you through the process on a call."

**"What does it cost my brides?"**
> "During beta, complimentary for the bride and up to two members of her party. We cover everything — the nurse, the formula, the setup. The only thing your bride needs to do is be there."

**"I'd need to vet you before recommending anything."**
> "I'd expect nothing less. That's exactly what the call is for — ask me anything. We can also do a complimentary demo session for you personally so you know exactly what your brides will experience."

## Outreach Sequence: 5 Calls in 2 Weeks

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Send video DMs to 10 planners (LinkedIn + Instagram split) |
| Day 3 | Follow up in thread: "Wanted to bump this up — any interest?" (one line, no re-pitch) |
| Day 5 | Final follow-up to non-responders: "Totally understand if timing is off — leaving the door open if it becomes relevant." Then stop. |
| Day 7 | Send second wave to 10 new planners |
| Day 10 | Repeat follow-up cadence on second wave |

3 responses out of 20 outreaches is a solid conversion rate for cold video DMs. Five calls likely requires 15–25 sends. Don't over-optimize the script — optimize the volume and the personalization hook.

---

## Why This Script Works

- **Specific over flattery** — one real detail about their work opens more doors than any compliment
- **"Three spots"** — genuine scarcity, stated plainly, not as an urgency tactic
- **"Before anyone else has it"** — exclusivity framing that appeals to planners who compete on differentiation
- **15-minute ask** — the lowest-friction yes possible
- **No price mentioned** — removes the money objection entirely; they only need to say yes to a call
